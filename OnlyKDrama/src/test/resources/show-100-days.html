Synopsis

Yi Yul, Crown Prince of Joseon, is a perfectionist who disregards most royal palace nobles. His cold and demanding demeanor masks deeply rooted loneliness. He passes a law stating that citizens of marriageable age must wed before the age of 28.

After an attempt on his life, Yi Yul is severely injured and finds himself in the care of the Hong family, a hardworking father and daughter. He’s experiencing temporary amnesia, and now unaware of his identity, he is free to wander at will as a commoner.

Hong Sim is a strong, intelligent woman with a secret past. As the oldest unmarried woman in the village, she is forced to marry quickly or become an unwilling concubine of the corrupt Lord Park. Due to the law he himself passed, Yi Yul must marry Hong Sim or face punishment from officials.

Can their budding romance withstand Yi Yul’s eventual recovery with his memory fully intact?
